1. Introduction

eBrand Promotion respects your privacy. This policy explains how information submitted through our enquiry platform is collected and handled.

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect:

3. How We Use Your Information

We use submitted information to receive, review, manage and respond to client enquiries.

4. Email Notifications

Enquiry details may be sent to the authorised eBrand Promotion sales team through email notifications to support communication and follow-up.

5. Data Storage and Security

Enquiry information is intended to be stored securely using our database and file-storage services. Access is intended to be restricted to authorised personnel.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the platform.

7. Data Retention

Information will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for handling enquiries, business operations and applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable requirements.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact: sales@ebrandpromotion.com
